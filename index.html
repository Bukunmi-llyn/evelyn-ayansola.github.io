How I work

Start with the user, not the solution

Every product decision traces back to research. I build what discovery shows is needed, not what seems logical.

Prioritise hard, ship something meaningful

I decide what ships now and what waits, and I say what I gave up. The MVP should earn trust before it earns scale.

Keep the team aligned, not just informed

On a lean team, ambiguity is expensive. I invest in shared goals, clear ownership and documented decisions.

Show, don't tell

With institutional buyers, a working demo beats a strategy deck. I get something in front of decision-makers early.
